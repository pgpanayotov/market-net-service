# P4: Ingestion worker (2026-10-18)

Phase log for P4, written in its own session in parallel with P5 (API). The main session merges it into `engineering-notes.md`.

## What was built

**Binance adapters** (`src/MarketData.Infrastructure/Binance/`, Binance.Net 13.7.0):
- `BinanceEndpointOptions` (section `MarketData:Binance`):
  - `RestBaseUrl` (default `https://api.binance.com`) and `SocketBaseUrl` (default `wss://stream.binance.com:9443`), validated on start.
  - Alternatives where the official hosts are blocked: `https://data-api.binance.vision` and `wss://data-stream.binance.vision`.
  - `ToEnvironment()` builds a custom `BinanceEnvironment`; the futures addresses keep their live values.
- `KlineMapper`, the one mapping used by both paths: `ToCandle(symbolId, IBinanceKline, receivedAt)` for WS and REST, and `TryMapClosed` (WS only), which accepts only `Final` 1m klines.
- `IMarketDataStream` / `BinanceMarketDataStream`:
  - One `SubscribeToKlineUpdatesAsync(symbol, OneMinute)` per symbol; Binance.Net combines them on one socket.
  - Subscribe and unsubscribe are idempotent per symbol (`UpdateSubscription.CloseAsync`).
  - `ConnectionLost` / `ConnectionRestored` fire once per socket, not once per subscription.
  - `IsConnected` is true when every subscription's socket is connected.
  - Subscriptions are tied to the stream's lifetime token, because Binance.Net closes a subscription when the token passed to Subscribe is cancelled.
- `IKlineRestClient` / `BinanceKlineRestClient`:
  - `GetClosedCandlesAsync(symbol, range)` yields pages of ≤1000 klines, never the still-open minute, and stops on a short page.
  - On 429/418 it honours `Retry-After` (delta or date); without the header it waits 60s on 429 and 2 min on 418. The wait is a shared "blocked until" that holds back every caller, not just the request that failed.
  - Transient failures (5xx, network) retry with backoff, at most 4 times. Permanent errors throw `KlineRequestException`.
- `AddBinanceMarketData(configuration)` (DI) and `ExponentialBackoff` (shared helper with jitter).

**Ingestion host** (`src/MarketData.Ingestion`, now on `Microsoft.NET.Sdk.Web`):
- `LeaderLock` (hosted service):
  - Takes `pg_try_advisory_lock(MarketData:LeaderLockKey)` on a dedicated, non-pooled session (`application_name=marketdata-leader`).
  - On a standby it retries every `LeaderRetryInterval` (10s).
  - On the leader it probes the session every `LeaderCheckInterval` (5s). A failed probe, or any leader service ending, ends the term: the role flips to standby first, then every service is cancelled and awaited, and closing the session releases the lock.
  - Leader-only work implements `ILeaderService.RunAsync(leadershipToken)`. These services are singletons that LeaderLock starts per term; they are not hosted services.
- `SymbolChangeListener : PgNotificationListener, ILeaderService`: LISTEN `symbol_changes`. On every (re)connect it reconciles all symbols. A notification re-reads that symbol by name and subscribes or unsubscribes to match the DB; `op` is ignored.
- `SymbolSubscriptionManager`: a serialized, idempotent reconcile (`GetActiveAsync` vs. `stream.Subscriptions`) and apply. A newly subscribed symbol gets a capped tail backfill (`SymbolAdded`).
- `BinanceStreamCollector`:
  - Hooks `CandleClosed` (records the lag metric and does `pipe.Write`, no I/O) and `ConnectionRestored` (reconnect metric and a tail backfill).
  - Reconciles at term start and then every `ReconcileInterval` (60s).
  - Drops every subscription when the term ends.
- `CandlePipe`: `Channel<Candle>`, bounded to `ChannelCapacity` (10k) with `DropOldest`. The drop callback counts the drop and requests a gap scan.
- `CandleWriter`:
  - Batches up to `WriteBatchSize` (500) rows or `WriteBatchWindow` (1s) into one `UpsertAsync`.
  - Retries forever (within the term) with exponential backoff capped at `WriteMaxRetryDelay` (30s). After recovering it requests a tail and a scan (`DbRecovered`).
  - Sends no `pg_notify`.
- `BackfillScheduler` + `GapBackfiller`:
  - Requests coalesce by (kind, symbol). They run in order: per-symbol tails, then all-symbol tails, then scans.
  - Tail: from `max(open_time) + 1m` to the last closed minute. A symbol with no data, or one being added, is capped at `MarketData:BackfillOnAdd` (24h).
  - Scan: `FindMissingRangesAsync` over `[now − GapScanWindow (48h), floor(now) − 1m)`, at term start, every `GapScanInterval` (5 min), and after drops or DB recovery.
  - Pages are upserted as they arrive. A failed request is queued again after a backoff.
- Health on `:8081` (`Kestrel:Endpoints:Health:Url`):
  - `/health/live` runs no checks.
  - `/health/ready` runs `database` (`CanConnectAsync`), `schema` (the existing `PendingMigrationsHealthCheck`) and `ingestion`.
  - `ingestion` on a standby: Healthy, role `standby`.
  - `ingestion` on the leader: WS connected, listener `IsListening`, and every active symbol's newest candle closed within `FreshnessThreshold` (2 min), with that much grace after the term starts or the symbol is subscribed.
  - The JSON body is `{status, role, checks[]}`.
- OpenTelemetry metrics on the existing `MarketData` meter (`IngestionMetrics`):
  - `marketdata.candles.ingested{symbol}`
  - `marketdata.ingest.lag` (s, histogram)
  - `marketdata.ws.reconnects`
  - `marketdata.backfill.candles{symbol,reason}`
  - `marketdata.channel.depth` (gauge)
  - `marketdata.channel.dropped`
  - `marketdata.writer.failures`
  - `marketdata.gap.minutes{symbol}` (gauge)
  - `marketdata.worker.is_leader` (gauge)
  - The P3 listener metrics, plus HttpClient instrumentation.
  - Export: OTLP when `OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_ENDPOINT` is set; console when `MarketData:Telemetry:ConsoleExporter=true`.
- Options: `IngestionOptions` is bound from `MarketData` (`BackfillOnAdd`, `ChannelCapacity`, `WriteBatchSize`, `WriteBatchWindow`, `WriteMaxRetryDelay`, `GapScanInterval`, `GapScanWindow`, `ReconcileInterval`, `LeaderRetryInterval`, `LeaderCheckInterval`, `LeaderLockKey`, `FreshnessThreshold`). The connection string is `ConnectionStrings:MarketData` and is required.

**Tests** (`tests/MarketData.Ingestion.IntegrationTests`, 30 tests):
- Unit:
  - Golden files: `Golden/ws-kline-closed.json` (a combined-stream message parsed by Binance.Net's own serializer context) and `Golden/rest-klines.json` (served through a real `BinanceRestClient` over a stub `HttpMessageHandler`) map to **identical** `Candle`s. Every column is checked at full precision, and an open kline is not mapped.
  - REST client: 2500 minutes are paged as 1000/1000/500 with no gaps or overlap; the open minute is excluded; 429 waits for `Retry-After`; a 418 ban also delays later calls; a 400 isn't retried; an empty range sends no request. Fake time is used throughout.
  - `ExponentialBackoff` (doubling, cap, jitter bounds). `BackfillScheduler` (coalescing, run order, wait/timeout).
- Integration (the real `Program` via `WebApplicationFactory`, Testcontainers `postgres:17-alpine`, `FakeMarketDataStream`, `StubKlineRestClient` with deterministic synthetic klines):
  - Streamed candles are persisted exactly.
  - Symbols with no data get a startup backfill capped at `BackfillOnAdd`.
  - A mid-series hole is repaired by the scan: the stub saw a request for exactly the hole range, and `FindMissingRangesAsync` is then empty.
  - A reconnect triggers a tail backfill.
  - Readiness: the leader is ready; it reports 503 "WebSocket disconnected" while the stream is down and recovers after; it fails for stale symbols; live returns 200.
  - `symbol_changes` add (insert + `pg_notify` in one tx) subscribes and backfills; remove unsubscribes and keeps the history.
  - Stale or duplicate hints are checked against the DB.
  - Changes made while no worker runs are applied by the reconcile on connect.
  - A second worker stays standby (no subscriptions, ready=200, role `standby`). After `pg_terminate_backend` on the lock holder it takes over, and the old leader demotes and unsubscribes.

## Decisions and deviations

- **No `candles_written` NOTIFY, and readiness reads freshness from the DB.** The plan's P4 test bullet still says "candles persisted and notified"; that predates the P3 decision, so the writer sends nothing. For freshness, readiness uses `GetLatestOpenTimesAsync` (one index probe per symbol) instead of an in-memory `lastCandleAt`, so candles stored by backfill count too and there is no writer state to keep in sync.
- **Leader services are run by `LeaderLock`, not by the host.** Each term starts fresh service runs with a term token, and a leader that loses its session goes back to standby instead of exiting. Between the session dying and the old leader noticing (≤ `LeaderCheckInterval`), two workers may write; that's harmless because upserts are idempotent.
- **Retries are hand-rolled with exponential backoff and jitter, not Polly.** This matches the P3 listener and needs no new package; Polly.Core is only a transitive dependency. Nothing was added to `Directory.Packages.props`.
- **Options class is `BinanceEndpointOptions`, not `BinanceOptions`.** The latter clashes with `Binance.Net.Objects.Options.BinanceOptions`. The config key `MarketData:Binance:RestBaseUrl` matches P5's (`ExchangeInfoSymbolValidator`). Both sessions call `services.AddBinance(...)` with the same environment; the main session may want one shared helper at merge time.
- **Tail backfill cap:** the 24h cap applies when a symbol has no data or is (re)subscribed (`SymbolAdded`). Startup and reconnect tails for symbols with data are uncapped from `max(open_time) + 1m`, so a worker that was down for days catches up fully.
- **Gap scan upper bound** is `floor(now) − 1m` (exclusive), the same as the ADR's `date_trunc('minute', now()) − 2 minutes` inclusive. The newest minute is left to the WebSocket.
- **Metric names are OTel style** (`marketdata.*`, following P3) instead of system-design §7's Prometheus names. `channel_depth`, `gap_minutes` and `worker_is_leader` are gauges; `channel.dropped` and `writer.failures` were added. The main session may want to update §7.
- **Not done (ADR-005 action item 3, second half):** "readiness fails if `gap_minutes > 5` for more than 10 min". The gauge exists, but readiness doesn't use it yet. Freshness already catches a dead feed, and the scan repairs holes within one interval in practice.
- **Unwritten candles at shutdown** (still in the pipe or a failing batch) aren't flushed. The next leader's startup tail and scan recover them.
- **`Microsoft.Extensions.Hosting`** dropped from the Ingestion csproj (the Web SDK provides it). The worker test project gained `Microsoft.AspNetCore.Mvc.Testing` (already centrally versioned). Lock files updated.
- **Binance.Net's REST rate limiter also honours `Retry-After`, in real time.** The unit tests turn it off (`RateLimiterEnabled = false`) so they check the adapter's own wait on fake time. In production both are active; the adapter waits first, so the library adds no further delay.

## Validation (actually run)

- `dotnet build MarketData.slnx`: 0 warnings, 0 errors.
- `dotnet test --solution MarketData.slnx`: **168 passed** (97 Core + 41 Infrastructure + 30 Ingestion; the Api test project has none yet on this branch). The Ingestion project was also run 3 more times in a row: 30/30 each time, about 15–21s per run.
- **Live Binance run**:
  - Setup: `postgres:17-alpine` in Docker on :55432, `dotnet ef database update`, then `dotnet run --project src/MarketData.Ingestion` with `ConnectionStrings__MarketData` and `Kestrel__Endpoints__Health__Url=http://127.0.0.1:18081`. The default endpoints `api.binance.com` and `stream.binance.com:9443` were used.
  - Startup: became leader and backfilled 1440 candles per symbol (24h cap, empty DB). Subscribed ADAUSDT/BTCUSDT/ETHUSDT on one socket. `/health/ready` was Healthy, role `leader`.
  - **One row per symbol per minute:** 16:57–17:00 UTC each had exactly 3 rows from the WS, with `received_at − close_time` ≈ 0.49–0.55s.
  - **Kill for 5 minutes:** the process was force-killed at 17:01:30 and restarted at 17:06:38. The startup tail backfilled 5 candles per symbol (17:01–17:05). Each symbol then had 1450 contiguous rows (16:57 the day before → 17:06), and the ADR-005 gap-scan query returned **0 missing minutes**.
  - **Runtime add/remove:** an `INSERT` of SOLUSDT plus `pg_notify('symbol_changes', …)` in one transaction (what the API will do) gave, within a second, "Symbol change notified", a subscription, and "Backfilled 1440 candles for SOLUSDT (Tail, SymbolAdded)"; the 17:07 SOL candle arrived from the WS with 0.60s lag. `is_active = false` plus a notify gave "Unsubscribed from SOLUSDT@kline_1m"; readiness stayed Healthy with 3 symbols and the history was kept.
  - **Failover:** a second process on :18082 reported `standby` (Healthy, no subscriptions). After a force-kill of the leader at 17:08:36, it acquired the lock at 17:08:44, subscribed all 3 symbols and was ready as `leader`. The 17:08 candles arrived from its WS (0.55s lag), and the gap query was still 0.
  - The container was removed afterwards.
